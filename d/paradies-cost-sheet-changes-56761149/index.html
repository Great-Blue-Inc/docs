4What are “Type” and “Status”? Answered

When we're in the builder what are “type” and “status”?

Type says whether the sheet is the original Quote or a Production copy made after the order comes in. It is set when the sheet starts.

Status says where the sheet stands. It is Draft until it is finalized, then Quoted. A production copy shows its draft status as Draft (PO stage).
