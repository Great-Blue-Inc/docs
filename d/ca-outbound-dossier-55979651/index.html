How to use this

Every card carries the research, the opening hook, and the 1-2 offerings to pitch. Bid Quoter leads wherever the company bids work; pitch only the matched offerings on the card, never the full catalog. Fill the draft template with the card's hook line, verify the company on its CSLB link, and send from your own address. All outreach is human-sent, one at a time.

Confidence marks the research, not the lead: a low means searches surfaced little (often just no web presence, common for solid small shops) - call rather than email those.
