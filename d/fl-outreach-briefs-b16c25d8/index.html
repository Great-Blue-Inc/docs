How to use this

Same rules as the California page: Bid Quoter leads wherever the company bids work; pitch only the matched offerings on the card, never the full catalog. Fill the draft with the card's hook line and send from your own address, one at a time, human-sent. Florida's license extract carries no phone numbers, so the website (or the DBPR license lookup by name) is the contact path. Cards marked "promoted by research" were initially screened out only because Florida's public data hides company size; the research confirmed they're real multi-person operations.
