Requirement and solution

One question, one automation, no other stage changes.

Requirement Solution
"Is it possible to have Salesforce automatically update an Opportunity to Under Contract once both the client and I have completed the DocuSign signing process and the fully executed agreement has been returned to Salesforce?" When DocuSign reports an envelope completed, meaning every signer is done, and that envelope was sent from an Opportunity, the Opportunity moves to stage Under Contract automatically. Opportunities already Closed Won, Closed Lost, Not Bidding, or already Under Contract are skipped, so a completed envelope can never move a deal backward or reopen a closed one.
02

What changed

This automation is new. No prior version exists to compare against, so this section shows what runs today.

Before this build, a completed DocuSign envelope had no effect on the Opportunity's stage. Moving a deal to Under Contract after signing was a manual step.

The flow after

After. The automation in Flow Builder, version 2, Active. It starts on the DocuSign envelope object, checks that the envelope's source record is an Opportunity, looks that Opportunity up, confirms it was found, then checks whether its stage is eligible before updating it to Under Contract. Any record that fails a check along the way simply ends, with no error and no other side effect.

The trigger condition after

= Completed

After. The entry condition, boxed in red. The automation fires only when the envelope's status field changes and equals Completed, the value DocuSign writes back once every signer is done. Every other status change on the envelope is ignored.

The stage guard after

After. The decision that protects closed and already-contracted deals. The Opportunity's stage must not equal Closed Won, Closed Lost, Not Bidding, or Under Contract for the update to run. Any Opportunity already in one of those four stages is left exactly as it is.
