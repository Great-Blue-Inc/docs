01

Start a bid

Pick the spec PDF, check the pages, choose a run speed and start the run.

1Open Your spec books

Open Bid Quoting from Salesforce. It opens on Your spec books, a list of recent spec books and where each one stands.

Select Open on a book to go back to its draft. Select Upload a new spec to start a new one. Back to Salesforce, at the bottom of the left rail, leaves the app.

2Choose the spec PDF

Under Upload a spec, select Choose a PDF, or drop the file on the box.

Upload the Division 27 and 28 products section of the spec, not the whole project manual. A smaller, targeted section reads faster and matches better.

3Check the pages the app found

The app reads the file and finds the Division 27 and 28 pages by itself. It shows the page range and the number of sections it found.

Select Use these pages to keep that range, or Process the whole file instead. To use other pages, type a range such as 12-40, 88-95 and select Use this range.

4Pick the run speed

Standard is the default and costs less. Fast returns the draft sooner and costs more. Pick Fast only when the quote is needed soon.

5Start the upload

Select Start upload. The draft opens on this page when it is ready. It also stays in Your spec books, so it can be opened later.

02

While it runs

What the page shows during a run, and what to do meanwhile.

6Follow the steps

The strip at the top shows the run in order: Read, Find requirements, Match products, Price and Draft. A check mark shows each finished step. The pill beside them says In progress until the draft is ready.

Each step has an i icon. Hover over it to read what that step does.

7Leave and come back

There is no need to wait on the page. Close it and do other work. The book stays in Your spec books with In progress next to it. When it says Draft ready, select Open.

8If a run stops

If a run stops, the page says where it stopped. Select Resume processing. The run picks up where it stopped. Sections already read are kept, so the book does not start over.

03

The draft

Read each line, check its evidence, and fix what it asks for.

9Read the draft lines

Each line has a product, a quantity, a unit price, a line total and a status. The bar at the bottom keeps the running totals: priced material subtotal, labor estimate and draft total.

10Read the status pills

The Status column says what each line needs, with the reason under the pill.

Amber asks for a look or an entry, such as Enter footage, Enter price or Confirm quantity. Grey needs nothing, such as Priced from the price book. Green is a line that has been accepted.

Settings decides which price source comes first. A price from the other source shows amber, so it gets a check before it goes on a quote. In these pictures, the price book comes first, so a won-job price shows amber.

11Use the i icons

Hover over any i icon to see what a pill, a step or a total means. Here, the icon beside a won-job price explains where the price came from.

12Open a line and read its evidence

Select a product name to open the line in the panel on the right.

The Requirement tab shows the spec section, the spec text the line answers, the approved manufacturer and why this product matched. The quantity and unit price can also be changed here.

13See where the price came from

Price source says where the price came from, how it is priced, and how the product was picked.

14Swap in another product

Alternatives lists other close products with how well each one matches and its price. Select Pick this to swap one in.

15Enter a missing price

Type the price in the line’s Unit price box. The line total and the totals at the bottom update at once, and the status changes.

16Enter cable footage

A cable priced by the foot has no quantity until its footage is known, so it stays out of every total. Type the measured footage in the Qty box. Use the minus and plus buttons for small changes.

Footage can also come from the drawings. See Use footage in section 05.

17Accept a line

When a line looks right, select Accept line in its panel. Select Flag for review to mark it for a second look instead.

18Turn on Guided mode

New to the app? Turn on Guided mode in the left rail. It walks through the review one step at a time. Select Next to move on or Exit to close it.

04

Needs your call

One list of everything that waits on a person.

19Open Needs your call

Needs your call in the left rail gathers everything waiting on a decision, in groups: Confirm match, Enter footage, Enter price and Confirm quantity. Only groups with something in them show. The number beside each heading is what is left in it.

Select Quantity only to see only the quantities to confirm. Select the i beside a heading to read what that group needs.

20Collapse and expand the groups

Select a group heading to fold it. Select Collapse all to fold every group and see only the headings. Expand all opens them again.

21Confirm or replace a match

Each Confirm match item shows the spec requirement, the closest catalog product and how well it matches. The app found close products here but did not choose one.

Select Pick this to put that product on the draft. Select Search catalog to find a different product. Select Skip to move past it for now. Items under Enter footage and Enter price have Open, which opens the line.

05

Drawing counts

Count devices from the drawing PDF, check the counts, and put them on draft lines.

22Attach the drawing PDF

Open Drawing counts in the left rail and select Attach drawing PDF. The app reads the text printed on the drawing and counts every device label, using the drawing’s own legend. Single-letter symbols still need a person.

23Read the device cards

In the List view, each device type gets one card, with the total counted and the number of sheets. The card names the suggested draft line. To use a different line, type in the filter box and pick it from the list. Ask again asks the catalog again.

Nothing is placed on a line until Place counts on line is pressed.

24Accept or reject each count

The table under the cards has one row per device symbol per sheet. Check each row against the drawing, then select Accept or Reject. Accept all, above the cards, accepts every row at once.

A row marked Needs Review means the two counting passes did not agree. It stays out of every total until a person accepts or rejects it.

25Place the counts on a line

On the device card, select Place counts on line. All the sheets for that device go on one draft line, with the total. The card then says how many were placed and on which line. Select Change to pick a different line.

26Override the line for one row

Some rows keep their own picker in the Override line column, for example outlets and access points that also carry an estimated footage. Pick a draft line there, then select Use count to put that row’s count on it.

27Use footage for cable

Est. footage is an estimate made from the count. To use it, pick a cable line under Override line, then select Use footage. That sets the cable line’s quantity to the footage. Nothing changes until Use footage is pressed.

28Check the Map view

Select Map to see the counts grouped by sheet and by the room the drawing named. Each device type has its own color. A dashed outline means that count is not accepted yet. This view is not the drawing itself.

29Add a product from the drawings

When the legend names a device and the spec names its maker, the card shows a From the drawings proposal: the product, why it fits and the sheets the symbol is on. Select Add as new line to add it to the draft with the count.

30Check the new line

The card now says the count is placed on the new line. The line appears at the end of Draft lines with the drawing count as its quantity. It still needs a price.

When another card proposes the same product, it offers Place counts on that line instead of adding a second line.

31When the spec names no maker

The app does not guess. When the spec names no maker for a device, the card says so, and the product has to be picked by hand. It does the same when a symbol is only one character, or when the legend does not define it.

06

Create the quote

Choose the account, create the quote, and know what locks.

32Choose the account

In the bottom bar, type at least two letters of the account name and pick it from the list. The header shows the account at once. Create quote stays grey until an account is chosen.

33Create the quote

Select Create quote. If a cable line still has no footage, the app says so first, because that line will not be on the quote. Select Create without it to go on, or Go back to enter the footage.

34Open or download the quote

The bar now reads Quote created, with Open the quote and Download CSV. The pill in the step strip also reads Quote created.

35What locks

Once the quote exists, the draft is read-only. A note at the top says so, and every box and button on the draft is locked. Make later changes on the quote in Salesforce.

36No price and No footage

On the locked draft, a line that had no price reads No price. It adds nothing to the totals. A cable line that had no footage reads No footage. It was left off the quote, so add it on the quote in Salesforce.

07

Answers

Short answers to the questions that come up most.

What do the colors mean?

Amber asks for a look or an entry. Grey needs nothing. Green is a line that has been accepted. On the Map view, a dashed outline is a count that is not accepted yet. The line with a tinted background is the one open in the panel.

What happens to lines left off the quote?

A cable line with no footage is left off, and the app says so before the quote is made. A line with no price goes on the quote with no price and adds nothing to the totals. A rejected drawing count stays out of every total. Add anything that was left off on the quote in Salesforce.

Who confirms what?

The app reads the spec, matches products and drafts the lines. The estimator confirms the matches, quantities and prices, accepts or rejects each drawing count, and places counts on lines. Nothing from the drawings goes on a line until Place counts on line, Use count or Add as new line is pressed.

Does the app ever choose a product it is not sure of?

No. When the spec names no maker, when a symbol is one character, or when the legend does not define a symbol, the app proposes nothing. Close catalog products that it would not choose go to Confirm match.

Can a quote be changed after it is created?

Not from the app. The draft is read-only after Create quote. Open the quote and change it in Salesforce.

Where do Spec facts and Drawing facts fit?

Spec facts lists the rules that govern the whole job, such as approved manufacturers. Drawing facts shows the text read from each sheet: the sheet, the room and the symbol. Both are in the left rail.

Is the spec PDF kept?

No. The page says so beside Spec PDF: the file is read and not kept.
