What changed

Same production copy, CS-SEA-1580Y Splatter Paint Jellyfish Girls Tee, before and after the deploy.

read only

Before. The Samples card, outlined in red, carried a Locked tag and read-only rows (Buyer M 2, Control M 1). Screenshot from the request.

editable

After. The same copy in production, opened as the requester. The Samples card now carries the Editable tag, with a recipient, size and quantity control per row, Remove, and Add sample allocation. Packing and Freight stay locked. Nothing was saved during this check.

The help text on the Samples heading now reads "Sample pieces by recipient and size, carried from the quote. Edit them here if they changed." Samples feed no price or total, so editing them does not change any price on the copy.
