Why the Referred By report shows ID numbers

The report is working correctly. It shows exactly what is stored on the records — and on 162 opportunities what is stored is an ID instead of an act name. Here is the cause, the correction, and the cleanup.

What is happening

The Referred By Roll up field should hold a readable value, such as Repeat Client or the name of the act a customer saw. On 162 opportunities it holds an 18-character ID instead.

On every one of those records that ID is the exact ID of the act in the Act They Saw Heard field. The name it should be showing sits on the linked act record.

Where it comes from

The Lead Source Roll Up flow builds this value. Inside it, a calculation named formReferredByRollup decides what to store. Its act branch stores the act’s ID rather than the act’s name.

It is not limited to editing the referral fields. On any opportunity that already has an act selected, saving any unrelated change re-runs this calculation and replaces a good name with the ID. That is why most affected records were last touched by the automation user rather than by a person.

The correction

One line in that same calculation. Everything else in the flow is untouched.

Tested before and after

Why the formula field returned blank

Salesforce does not allow a text formula field to be used as a report grouping. Grouping on Referred By Rollup returns nothing whatever the formula contains. The report needs to group on the stored field, which reads correctly once the flow is corrected and the records are updated.

The cleanup

Correcting the flow stops new records from being affected. The 162 records already holding an ID need their value rewritten. Every one can be restored, because the correct act name is still on each record.
