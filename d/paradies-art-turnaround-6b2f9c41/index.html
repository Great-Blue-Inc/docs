122 requests created since 1 July 2026. All day figures are medians, so half of each group came in faster than the number shown and half slower.

What this shows

  • Three in four requests finish after the date first asked for, by about eight days.
  • Complexity matters more than decoration type. A hard screen print takes 46 days, a medium one 18.
  • Embroidery is asked for fastest and runs longest. Four days requested, seventeen actual.
  • Work sent back for revision adds about six days.
  • Claude already judges complexity when a request arrives, so this is known at the moment a due date gets set.

What we could do with it

  • Claude suggests a due date on the request, the way it suggests an artist today. Anyone can override it.
  • Show the typical time for that kind of work, and let whoever sets the date judge for themselves.
  • Flag a request when the date asked for sits well under the typical time.
  • Record art style too, so within a couple of months the estimates can account for that as well.
  • Leave it as is and look again once more work has closed.

Worth knowing

  • Some rows rest on five or six requests, so read those as early direction rather than settled numbers.
  • Figures cover requests created since 1 July, when the date tracking went live. They get steadier as more work closes.
