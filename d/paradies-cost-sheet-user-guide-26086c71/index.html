01

Start a cost sheet

Sandbox

A cost sheet always belongs to a Development Item.

  1. Open the Development Item.
  2. Find the Cost sheets card. Click Open cost sheet builder.
  3. Click New cost sheet.
  4. Turn on Guided mode if you want the builder to walk you through each step. Guided mode does not change the builder. It adds a card that tells you what to do next and stops you when a required field is empty.
Where the item details come from

Design ID, Padneck ID, blank style, description, colour and the comp come from the Art Request. The customer comes from the Development Item's account. The builder copies them when the sheet is created. Hover the (i) next to any field to see its source and how it is used.

02

The five pages

Same order as the workbook, top to bottom.

Page What you enter Workbook equivalent
1 · Header & mode Garment type. Pricing mode. MOQ. Margin target (starts at 35). Blank cost and vendor. Freight per piece. The header block and the blank line
2 · Decoration One line per print: location, colours, name drops, treatments. Applied elements (patch, hem tag) with their own cost. Print and treatment rows
3 · Tiers & sizes One row per quantity you want to price. The quoted price for each. Size upcharges for extended sizes. The quantity columns and the size rows
4 · Samples, packing & other Sample pieces by recipient and size. Standard packing bundle on or off. Packing add-ons. Other costs, per piece or one time. Packing rows and the notes
5 · Quote summary A name for the sheet. Estimated quantity. Check the figures. Accept the quote. The totals at the bottom

The builder saves as you go. Click Save draft on any page to stop and come back later.

03

Pricing modes

Pick the mode on page 1. It decides which calculator runs.

Mode What it prices What you must enter
Full Package Paradies buys the blank, decorates and packs. Cost = blank + decoration + treatments + packing + freight + other. Blank cost
Contract The customer supplies the goods. Paradies decorates only. Cost comes from one cell of the contract grid: the quantity band row and the colour count column. Decoration line and quantity
Third Party An outside vendor makes the finished item. Cost = vendor price + freight and duty per piece. Paradies adds margin. Vendor finished cost
04

How the price is built

Every figure is per piece. Every rate comes from the rate set. 

total cost      = blank + decoration + treatments + packing + freight + other
suggested sell  = total cost ÷ (1 − margin target), rounded up to the next $0.05
margin est.     = (quoted price − total cost) ÷ quoted price

Decoration, screen print detail

  • Print run. Pieces per hour for the colour count. Print cost per hour divided by pieces per hour.
  • Setup. Minutes per screen, plus extra minutes from the third screen. Times the setup rate. Divided by the quantity. This is why the cost per piece falls as the quantity rises.
  • Ink. One rate per location. Screen-print locations 2 to 4 get the location discount. Digital Squeegee uses its own ink rate and adds five screens.
  • Name drops. Each adds name-drop minutes to setup.
  • Treatments. The treatment rate, times hits. Foil = flat charge + per-inch charge × (height + 1).

Why 35.1 and not 35

4.64 ÷ 0.65 = 7.1385. The workbook rounds the sell price up to the next $0.05, so the price is $7.15. At $7.15 the margin earned is 35.1%. The builder shows the earned margin, not the target. Hover the (i) on Margin est. to see the formula.

05

The rate set

Every number the workbook kept in a hidden cell is now a record you can open and edit.

How it carried over high level

A rate set is one version of the workbook's rate tables. It holds rate lines. Each rate line is one number the workbook used: a print speed, an ink rate, a treatment charge, a packing price. Version 23 of the workbook became rate set RS-0000, 228 lines. Only one rate set is Active at a time. New cost sheets always price from the Active one.

What the rate set controls detail

Category Lines What the lines hold
Print 15 Pieces per hour and print cost per hour, per garment type. Setup minutes per screen and the extra minutes from the third screen. Setup rate under and over six screens. Name-drop minutes. Minimum print cost. Cost per colour. The volume tier threshold and its speed.
Ink 7 One ink rate per location: front, back, front pocket, sleeve, spine. The Digital Squeegee ink rate.
Treatment 19 One rate per treatment: high density, flock, dry and wet glitter, glitter flock, heat transfer, pad print transfer, custom label, top load, dye fee. Foil's three lines: flat charge, per-inch charge, the inch divisor. Contract treatments.
Packing 31 The standard bundle per garment type: fold, box, pallet, price ticket. Every add-on price: poly bags, size strip, hangtag, sticker, hanger, hat and tee combo. Contract finishing prices.
Contract grid 140 The contract decoration charge by quantity band and colour count. DS by band. DTF by band and square inches. Embroidery by band and stitch count. Adders and colour change.
Rush 4 Rush charges for 1, 2, 3 and 4 days.
Digitizing 3 Digitizing per logo and per 1,000 stitches.
Rounding 1 The $0.05 step the sell price rounds up to.
Other 8 DST edit and verification. The DTF formula. Royalties by licensor.

Change a rate procedure

  1. Open the Rate Sets tab. Open the Active rate set.
  2. Open the rate line you want to change. Edit the Value. Save.
  3. Price a test sheet. The new value is used at once.
What a rate change does not touch

An accepted quote keeps the rate set it was quoted on. Its numbers never move. A production copy prices at the rate set that is Active on the day it is created.

06

Lists you maintain

Nothing in a dropdown is typed into the code. Each list has a home in Setup or on a tab.

List Where to change it
Packing add-ons The Packing Options tab. One record per add-on. Its price is a rate line.
Sizes Setup → Picklist Value Sets → Garment Size. Used for size upcharges and sample sizes.
Decoration locations The locations the Active rate set has an ink rate for. Add an ink rate line to add a location.
Garment types The garment types the Active rate set has print and packing rates for.
Treatments The treatments the Active rate set has a rate for.
Sample recipient · Cost type · Element type · Application method · Decoration type Setup → Object Manager → the field's picklist values. Add a value and it appears in the builder.
Price rules The Price rules panel on the Tiers page. A rule adjusts cost or sell automatically when its conditions match.
07

Drafts, accepted quotes, production copies

Three states. Each one answers a different question.

Draft

You can edit everything. Save on any page. Come back any time. A Development Item can hold as many drafts as you need.

Accepted quote

Click Accept quote on the summary page. The sheet freezes. It is stamped with the rate set, the person and the date. Nothing on it changes again. This is what the customer was quoted.

Production copy

When the PO arrives, click Create production copy on the accepted quote. The copy carries the quoted price. It re-prices the cost at today's rates. Enter the order quantity, ship date, PO, blank vendor and PO notes on it.

Primary

Tick the Primary box on one sheet. That sheet's estimated contract value becomes the Development Item's Amount. Tick another to move it. Leave all unticked while you compare options.

09

Differences from the workbook

54 workbook scenarios were priced in both places. 49 match to the cent. Five differ on purpose.

Case Workbook Builder Why
Two or three print locations 5.46 · 5.87 5.30 · 6.05 The workbook has a fixed discount per named location and never discounts Back. The builder gives locations 2 to 4 the location discount. Confirm which rule you want. Either is a rate line change.
Standard packing turned off 4.26 4.08 The workbook's packing checkboxes are not connected, so it still charges $0.18. The builder honours the switch.
Bundle plus five add-ons 4.65 4.59 The workbook adds a $0.06 sticker apply charge that was not selected. The builder charges only what is selected.
Everything on one sheet 7.19 8.03 Combines the two cases above.
