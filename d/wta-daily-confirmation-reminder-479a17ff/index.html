The September 11 failure was reproduced in the sandbox and the new flow recovered from it. Deployed to production September 17.

02

What changed

Before (production, days 5 to 10). One update per line item. Salesforce groups line items 200 at a time and runs the groups side by side. Two groups held the same event on September 11 and one timed out. The days 1 to 4 flow has the same shape and is also retired.

After (Daily Confirmation Reminder, canvas). Starts on the line item, one update, batch size 20. The fault path runs to the right: Retry Check, Bump Retry back into the update, then Log Fault on a second failure.

After (Update Line Item). Updates the line item that started the run. Sets Refresh Talent Notes and Reset Event Status, the same two flags the old flows set.

03

Proof and deploy state

All runs in the sandbox on test line items only, under a test product and a test inbox. Nothing touched production.

  • Normal runs. 65 of 65 and 40 of 40 eligible line items updated across split batches. 0 faults.
  • Second-failure path. A forced update failure on 30 line items: each retried, failed again, logged one row. 0 error emails.
  • The September 11 failure, reproduced. The parent event was locked by another process for 55 seconds while the old flow shape ran on schedule. It hit "unable to obtain exclusive access to this record", faulted, updated nothing, and sent the platform error email, the same one as September 11.
  • The fix, under the same lock. The new flow ran on schedule with the parent locked. All 5 line items hit the lock on the first attempt, retried, and updated once the lock cleared. 0 faults, no error email. Repeated 5 more times in a harness: 5 of 5 recovered.
  • Production. Deployed September 17 at 10:20 AM ET on Jeff's approval. The new flow is active; the days 1 to 4 and days 5 to 10 flows are deactivated. First run is 1:00 AM September 18.
  • Open item. A second failure writes an Error Handling Log row, and your alert flow emails one message per row. On a night like September 11 that would be 42 emails. Your call: skip our rows in the alert, or drop the log row.
  • Rollback. Reactivate the two old flows and deactivate the new one. No data changes to undo.
