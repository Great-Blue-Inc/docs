Texting: costs, coverage, and ownership

Every figure and claim carries an inline reference[n] to the exact page or code it was verified against. The full reference list is at the bottom.

What does a number cost?

$1.15 a month for a local number[1], $2.15 for toll free[1]. One number, or one per salesperson — the count is a setup choice, not a pricing tier.

Source Twilio's US SMS pricing page — phone numbers section: local long code $1.15/mo, toll-free $2.15/mo.

What are the texting rates? Unlimited or per message? Incoming, outgoing, or both?

Per message, both directions billed. Twilio's list rate is $0.0083 per SMS each way[1]. US carriers add a compliance fee on outbound (roughly $0.0025–$0.007 by carrier[1]), so a sent text lands around 1.1–1.5 cents; received texts stay at the base rate.

One-time registration with the carriers: $4.50 business registration[2] plus a $15 campaign verification[4]. Ongoing: about $1.50–$10 a month in campaign fees depending on type[3] (the low-volume tier that fits this use is $1.50[4]).

For something like a few hundred texts a month, the whole program is roughly $10–$15 a month all in, including the number (computed from the referenced rates[1][4]).

Sources US SMS pricing page ($0.0083 both directions; per-carrier fee table) · A2P brand registration doc ("A one-time fee of $4.50 is required when registering a new Brand") · A2P campaign registration doc (monthly campaign fees) · Twilio pricing & fees article ($15 campaign verification; $1.50/mo low-volume).

SMS, MMS, RCS, WhatsApp?

SMS and MMS are both supported (MMS is $0.022 to send, $0.0165 to receive[1]). Twilio also offers WhatsApp[5] and RCS[6] as channels on the same platform. The matching engine keys on the phone number and the message text, so those channels can be added later as their own piece of work if wanted — nothing about today's build blocks them.

Sources US SMS pricing page (MMS rates) · Twilio WhatsApp · Twilio RCS.

Does texting work on our cell phones, or only inside Salesforce?

Clients: any phone. They text the business number like any contact, and it lands in Salesforce and matches automatically. Nothing to install on their side.

One boundary worth stating plainly: a text sent from a personal cell phone directly to a client's number (not through the business number) is invisible to any system — Twilio's or anyone else's. Capture requires the message to travel through the business number.

If it's a Twilio account — can we manage it ourselves (add or remove a user)?

Yes. The account would be WTA's. You can add or remove console users[9] and phone numbers[10] yourselves from the Twilio console; billing goes on WTA's card. We do the initial setup and wire it to Salesforce; day-to-day account administration stays in your hands.

Source Twilio: add and manage users in Console · Twilio: buy/release numbers from Console.
