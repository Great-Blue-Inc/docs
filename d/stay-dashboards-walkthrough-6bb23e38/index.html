04

What was built behind the boards

Live in prod

Two flows keep account status current. Two one-time passes filled in the data the charts needed.

Flow 1, account status from opportunities closed lost and disqualified

Runs when someone sets an opportunity to Closed Lost or Disqualified. It looks up the other opportunities on that account. If any are still open it changes nothing. If none are open it sets the account status to match the stage. It skips accounts already set to Customer.

Why the open-deal check matters

Without it, an account with a second live deal would be marked Closed Lost while still in play. The Customer guard stops an existing customer being downgraded when one deal is lost.

ElementDetail
TriggerOpportunity, on update, StageName = Closed Lost or Disqualified
CheckOther Opportunities on the account where IsClosed = false
ActionSets Account_status__c to Closed Lost or Disqualified to match the stage
GuardsSkips Account_status__c = Customer. Fault path cannot block the rep's save.

Flow 2, account status from sequences outreach

Runs when someone adds a contact to an Outreach sequence. It checks that contact's account status and sets it to Working only if the status is blank or New.

Why the blank-or-New condition matters

It stops the flow pulling an account backwards. An account already at In Opportunity or Nurture is left alone. Neither flow ever moves an account to an earlier status.

ElementDetail
TriggerContact, on create or update, Actively_Sequenced_in_Outreach__c = true
ConditionAccount_status__c is blank or New
ActionSets Account_status__c to Working

03

How the two boards connect

Same underlying list, same definitions, two audiences.

The short version for the call

  • Leaders see the whole picture, reps operate out of it. Every manager tile has a rep-level counterpart scoped to that person.
  • Touched means any logged activity since 8/5, the day the target list went live. Anything before that date sat on accounts chosen before the list existed.
  • List Rank is the priority order. The ranked tables are built to be worked top down.
  • Account status updates itself as deals close and contacts enter sequences, so the status charts stay accurate without manual upkeep.
  • Fit verdicts logged on the account record flow to both boards and into the next list refresh.
The one ask

Log fit verdicts and reasoning directly on the account record rather than in the sheet. Everything else on these boards updates on its own.

Reading the maps above reference

Each block in the two maps sits in the same position and relative size as the real tile on screen, so the layout above matches what opens in the browser. Letters and colours group tiles by the question they answer rather than by where they happen to sit.
