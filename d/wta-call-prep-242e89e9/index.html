01

Referred By roll up shows Ids

Fix proven in sandbox Decision open

Reported 8/25. Cause confirmed and answered in full 8/28. Production untouched.

Summary high level

The Referred By report grouped by Referred By Roll up and returned 18-digit Ids instead of act names. The cause is a single formula inside the lead source roll up flow. The correction is one line. Records already holding an Id need a separate cleanup, because the formula change only affects future saves.

259
Opportunities with an act set, the only ones that can be affected
162
currently holding an Id
93
visible in the report, capped by its date filter
1
line of metadata separating production from the fix

The mechanism low level

The flow contains two paths that write the same field. One is correct and has always worked. The formula is the outlier sitting beside it.

Flow elementWritesResult
Assign_Talent_In_Referred_By_Roll_Up$Record.Act_They_Saw_Heard__r.Namecorrect name
Assign_Referred_By_Roll_Up_Formula / _0 / _1formReferredByRollupraw Product Id

The formula is a priority ladder that takes the first populated source. Step two is the defect.

IF(NOT(ISBLANK(Referral_Contact_Name__c)),   formReferralContactandReferralAccount,
IF(NOT(ISBLANK(Act_They_Saw_Heard__c)),     Act_They_Saw_Heard__c,      <-- writes the Id
IF(NOT(ISPICKVAL(LeadSource,"")),           TEXT(LeadSource),
IF(NOT(ISBLANK(Refer_By_Text_temporary__c)), Refer_By_Text_temporary__c, ""))))

The correct name is written only when the act is first set. The rule logic on Talent_they_Saw_Heard is (1 AND 4 AND 5 AND 6) OR (2 AND 3), where conditions 2 and 3 mean the prior value was empty and the current value is populated. Every later save falls through to the formula, so a record starts correct and changes to an Id the next time the Opportunity is saved for any reason.

Two related points high level

  • Why the report shows fewer than 162. The Referred By report filters to events from today forward, so it is capped at 93. The other 69 are past events. Its report type is Opportunities_with_Products__c, a custom report type despite the standard-sounding name.
  • Referred By Rollup Report. That formula now copies Referred By Roll up directly, so it shows the same Id. It corrects itself once the flow is fixed and the records are cleaned up, with no separate change needed.
The cleanup carries the risk, not the formula

Correcting the 162 existing records means saving each one. Production carries 94 active flows plus a managed document trigger whose contents cannot be inspected. In an org that generates and sends contracts from Opportunity saves, that is not a safe blind batch. The sequence is: export the current values first, deploy the formula, save one low-risk record, check for any new documents, eSign records, email or calendar rows, then run the rest.

02

Invoice reminder alert links nowhere

Fix proven in sandbox Decision open

Alert forwarded 8/26. Cause found and the correction proven the same day. Production untouched.

The urgent half is the data, not the link

Three invoices have no Billing Contact. Those clients are receiving no payment reminders at all right now. Setting a Billing Contact on each restores their reminders immediately. The flow correction does not fix that on its own.

What happens high level

Every reminder in the daily flow sends to the invoice Billing Contact. When an invoice has no Billing Contact the send fails with "Recipient ID is required when using templates", and that failure produces the SFR alert. The alert then builds its record link from the same missing Billing Contact, so it has nothing to point at and arrives as a bare Salesforce address. It fails and links nowhere on exactly the occasions it fires.

This is long-standing rather than recent. The formula is unchanged since version 21, created September 2025.

The three invoices high level

The correction low level

One formula resource, ContactRecordLink, feeds all eight alert bodies, so this is a single edit rather than eight. The eight send actions are untouched, so nothing changes about who receives customer reminders.

before  ... & {!$Record.fw1__Contact__r.Id} & ...
after   ... & {!$Record.Id} & ...  {!$Record.Name}

Proven on a test invoice with no Billing Contact, using two temporary formula fields read back through a query. Same formula engine and a real empty value, with no flow run and no send action involved.

Optional improvement

The flow could skip invoices with no Billing Contact and go straight to the alert, rather than letting the send fail and retry five times a day. Available if wanted, not included in the correction above.

03

eSign contract build

Two questions open

Scope written and in contract drafting. Full document-action list circulated 8/26.

Settled high level

  • Wet sign. Edit restriction rather than an open password. A checkbox on the contract defaulting from one on the Account, its own email template, reminders that link a document the client can open, and the thanks-for-signing email once a returned copy is recorded.
  • All 16 document actions mapped, split into six sent for signature and seven generated only.
  • Migration includes the attached contract files, not only the records.
  • A daily copy of signed contracts off Salesforce stays a separate optional piece.

The two open questions low level

#Document actionQuestion
#8[Invoices] Invoice, built on the oPay objectCovered, or retired along with oPay?
#27[Proposals] With Pricing no Subtotal, EditableShould the Word output be covered as well?

Measured from the files actually generated: 561 PDF proposals in the last year against 2 Word ones, the most recent in May. Word output has no native path on the Salesforce platform, and an editable contract works against the edit-restriction requirement already agreed for wet sign. The recommendation is PDF only.

Facts behind the plan low level

  • Uninstalling Concord deletes the data. Roughly 12,000 documents and their attachments go with it, and the platform export is available for only two days. Migration has to run before any cancellation.
  • Storage is not a constraint. Signed PDFs average about 450KB, roughly 3.2GB a year against 43.5GB free.
  • Automatic counter-sign is the real journey. 8,559 automatic against 2 manual, all time.
  • Sending addresses. 66 verified addresses already exist in the org.

05

Calendar reliability

Closed and holding

Crash fixed 16 August. Clean on live traffic since.

What happened and what changed high level

The Saturday invite job hit a platform limit on the first batch ever to exceed 25 rows. The fix raised the existing recursion guard around the write-backs in all three queueable jobs, so the trigger cannot re-enter from inside a job. Deployed 16 August with 145 of 145 tests passing and the rollback source captured beforehand.

Proven three ways: red and green in sandbox on matching code, the exact failing load replayed clean, and live traffic since. The event links were confirmed against the calendar.

Why the three incidents were different low level

Three separate failure modes in one pipeline rather than one problem recurring. April was a duplicate-events race, resolved by deduplication. July was write-back loss under record lock, resolved by a retry and the 25-row cap. August was job chaining meeting an armed email flag on the first oversized batch. The April deduplication work is what contained August, since the 25 orphaned events were reused and no duplicate was ever visible to a user.
