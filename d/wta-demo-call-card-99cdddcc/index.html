01Intro (say once)

  • Demoing the opportunity email matching app we put together.
  • Each event is its own opportunity in Salesforce. A sender with one open opportunity matches on the email address, same as any provider.
  • The difference is a sender with multiple open opportunities. Match My Email needs a name tag in the subject to pick one. Ours reads the content, the event date, names from the opportunity, and the event type.
  • Suggestions come from logic we control, you give feedback, we refine it over time — including how much evidence it needs before filing automatically, a dial you own in settings.
  • Unsure cases go to a review queue, ranked candidates with reasoning.
  • Agenda: single-opportunity case, multiple-opportunity case, inbound and outbound, then texts.
  • Texts: webhook built, not connected yet. Twilio forwards to it, outbound possible through the same service.
  • Junk (OK / thanks) auto-filters into a Filtered section, keyword list you control in settings.
  • Storage: messages live directly in Salesforce as records you own. No cloud provider holding your client data before it gets wiped. Full control of your own data.
