How leads were found

  • Every Harvest customer has an account address of the form firmname.harvestapp.com.
  • About 3,500 firms that bill by the hour or project were checked. Each firm's address was guessed from its website name. Ex: kevindalyarchitects.harvestapp.com.
  • Harvest answers each address one of three ways. Redirect to the Harvest sign in page = active account. Redirect to a reactivate page = account on hold. Page not found = no account at that address.
  • Known Harvest users were checked before and after the run as a control. All passed. Made up addresses never return an account.
  • About 350 accounts found. Only addresses that are the firm's own distinctive name were kept. Short or common addresses were dropped because someone else could own them. That left 206 leads.
  • Contacts come only from each firm's own website (team, about and contact pages). Names must appear on the page. Emails and phones are only ones the site publishes. Nothing is guessed. Phones are the company main line.

How leads are ranked

  • Qualification is one thing only: a confirmed Harvest account.
  • Priority = 100 minus three penalties. Lowest score is 5.
  • Reach penalty (18 per step): direct email 0 · personal email for another person 18 · general inbox 36 · main line only 54 · nothing 72.
  • Role penalty (6 per step): Ops or finance (the person who runs time tracking) 0 · Other leader (owner, director, not ops) 6 · Exec, must forward (CEO or partner at a big firm who would pass it to the right team) 12 · no named person 18.
  • Harvest penalty: account active 4 · account on hold 0. On hold usually means the firm is leaving.
