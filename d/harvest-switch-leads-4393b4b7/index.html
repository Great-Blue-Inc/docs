Every Harvest customer gets an account address of the form firmname.harvestapp.com. Harvest answers a request to that address in one of three ways. The account is active. The account is on hold. Or no account exists. Blue Leads guessed the address for each firm from its website name and recorded the answer. No login was used and nothing was sent.

The method was checked against firms known to use Harvest before the run and again after it. Every check passed. Made up addresses never return a hit, so a hit is a real account.

Where to start

Work the on hold account first. Then the list in section 02. Most sites do not state headcount, so size is unknown for most leads. Firms that look like Denham Wolf convert best because the whole firm runs on Harvest. Skip ahead past names known to be very large. Ex: Deloitte or Grant Thornton. At a large firm the account is most likely one team.

03

Email drafts

One draft per group. Short. One real detail about the firm in the first line. Fill the highlighted parts before sending.

For firms whose Harvest account is on hold

Use for: African Library Project and any account that flips to on hold later. They are most likely mid switch.

Subject: Replacing Harvest

Hi {first name}

I saw {firm} may be moving off Harvest. A lot of teams are after the switch to usage pricing at renewal.

We built Blue Board for firms that bill by the hour or by the project. Time tracking. Project hours. Approvals. Invoicing. All in one place.

If you are still choosing a replacement I can set up a trial with your clients and projects already loaded.

{sender}

For firms on an active account

Use for: most of the list. Works best for firms shaped like Denham Wolf. Swap in one real detail from their site.

Subject: Harvest renewal at {firm}

Hi {first name}

{One line about their work. Ex: Saw the new library project in Oakland.}

Firms like yours have been getting renewal quotes from Harvest that are several times last year. The new pricing charges for projects and clients on top of seats.

Blue Board covers the same ground. Time tracking. Project hours and budgets. Approvals. Invoicing.

When does your Harvest plan renew? I can show you what the move would look like before then.

{sender}

For large firms (200+ staff)

Use for: the large firm list. The Harvest account is most likely one team or office. Find that team first.

Subject: Time tracking for {team or office}

Hi {first name}

Your team at {firm} may be one of the groups on Harvest. Its new usage pricing is landing hardest on teams with many active projects.

Blue Board is a drop in replacement for project time tracking and client invoicing. It can run for one team without a company wide rollout.

Worth a short look before your next renewal?

{sender}
Do not say

Never mention the account check or the harvestapp.com address. It reads as surveillance and kills the reply. Lead with the price change, which is public. Confirm any Blue Board feature or price claim with Josh before it goes out.

What lifts the reply rate

  • Send to the person who owns billing or operations. Ex: operations director, studio manager, or finance lead. Not a generic inbox.
  • Ask about the renewal date. It is the one question that matters to them and is easy to answer.
  • Offer a trial with their own clients and projects already loaded. Moving data is the main reason firms stay put.
  • Follow up once after a week with a single line. Stop after that.

Tiers

  • A scores 70 or more. B scores 50 to 69. C scores under 50.
  • Strong match means the address is the firm's own distinctive name. Ex: kevindalyarchitects. Only strong matches get a tier.
  • Needs a quick check means the address is short or a common word. Ex: jacobs, exponent, or cap. Someone else could own it. Confirm before outreach.
  • Set aside only for a site that did not answer or an address that could not be tied to the firm. Firm type, size and country never remove a lead.

Limits and next steps

  • An active account shows the firm pays Harvest today. It does not show the firm is unhappy. The renewal date decides that.
  • No contact names yet. Each lead has the firm, the site and the contact page. Finding the right person is the next step, by hand or with Apollo on this list only.
  • Firm type comes from site keywords and is rough. Treat it as a hint.
  • Firms whose Harvest address differs from their web name are missed. The real number of Harvest users in this list is higher than shown.
  • Leads are loaded to the Blue Board sandbox only. Production needs a separate go.
  • A monthly recheck of every account found will flag any that move to on hold. Those are the warmest leads this method can find.
