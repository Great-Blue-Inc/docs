04

Pages you have not seen yet

Samples

  • Add sample pieces by recipient and size. Add more than one row.
  • What to expect: the rows print on the cost sheet PDF and carry to the production copy.

Packing

  • The standard packing bundle is on by default. Turn it off and note the cost. Turn it on and compare.
  • What to expect: turning it off removes the packing cost. In your workbook the packing checkboxes do not change the cost, so a builder sheet with packing off will not match the workbook.
  • Below the bundle switch, add one or two add-ons (poly bags, size strip, hangtag, sticker, hanger, hat and tee combo) and watch the cost change.

Other costs

  • Add a cost line and choose a cost type.
  • Try Per piece: the amount is added to every piece.
  • Try One time: the amount is spread across the quantity.
  • What to expect: the per-piece cost changes in each case.

Summary and Finalize

  • Give the sheet a display name.
  • Estimated quantity starts at your MOQ. The sheet uses it for estimated contract value (estimated quantity times the price at that tier).
  • Check the figures and click Finalize cost sheet.
  • What to expect: the sheet freezes.

The finalized sheet

  • Open a sheet you finalized. It shows the date, the person, and the rate set it was priced on. Nothing on it can change.
  • Click View PDF to see the cost sheet PDF.
  • Click Create production copy.
  • What to expect: the copy keeps your quoted price and re-prices the cost at today's rates. If a rate changed since you finalized, the production copy shows the new cost.

The Primary checkbox

  • On the Development Item, each cost sheet has a Primary checkbox. New sheets are not primary.
  • Check it on one sheet. What to expect: that sheet's estimated contract value becomes the Development Item's amount.
  • Check a different sheet. What to expect: the amount moves to that sheet.
05

Contract mode

  • Contract is for jobs where the customer supplies the goods and you decorate only.
  • Start a new cost sheet, choose Contract, add a decoration line, and enter a quantity.
  • What to expect: the price comes from one cell in the contract grid, chosen by quantity band and colour count (or stitch count for embroidery, square inches for DTF).
  • Then price one real recent contract job in it and compare to what you quoted.
06

Third Party mode

  • Third Party is for jobs where an outside vendor makes the finished piece.
  • Start a new cost sheet, choose Third Party, enter the vendor's finished cost, and add freight and duty per piece if the job has them.
  • What to expect: cost is vendor price plus freight and duty, then margin is applied the same way as the other modes.
  • Then price one real recent third party job in it and compare to what you quoted.
07

The rate set

Every number the workbook kept in a hidden cell is now a record you can open and edit.

  • Open the Rate Sets tab: https://customization-fun-3784--dev2.sandbox.my.salesforce.com/lightning/o/Rate_Set__c/list
  • Open RS-0000 (version 23, Active). It holds every rate line: print speeds, ink rates, treatment charges, packing prices, and the contract grid.
  • Open one rate line, change the Value, and save. Price a test sheet.
  • What to expect: the new number shows right away.
  • A sheet you already finalized keeps the rate set it was priced on. A new sheet or a production copy prices at the active rate set.

Update rate lines from a spreadsheet

  • On the RS-0000 record page, find the Update rate lines panel.
  • Click Download template (.xlsx). Edit values in the file. Do not change the first column.
  • Under Where the prices come from, keep the default. Choose the file. Review the preview of what will change.
  • Click Apply.
  • What to expect: the changed lines update, and each change is logged on the rate set.

Known differences from your workbook

We ran 54 scenarios from your workbook through the builder. 49 match to the cent. The five that differ:

CaseWhat differsWhy
Two print locations (front and back)Builder is lowerThe workbook only discounts certain locations by name. The builder discounts the second through fourth location. This is question 5 in the email.
Three print locationsBuilder is higherSame rule.
Packing bundle turned offBuilder is lowerThe workbook's packing checkboxes do not change the cost. The builder's switch does.
Bundle on plus five add-onsBuilder is slightly lowerOne workbook add-on is tied to the wrong checkbox and adds a charge that was not selected. The builder charges only what you select.
Hoodie with two locations, foil, high density, add-ons, and freightBoth of the above combineSeveral effects show up together.
