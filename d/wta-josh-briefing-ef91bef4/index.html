What it is

  • A matching engine that runs entirely inside Salesforce. Client emails and texts attach themselves to the right opportunity automatically. Today it lives in a Great Blue development org; on engagement it deploys into WTA's own org.
  • It matches on the sender first, then reads the message itself when a client has several open opportunities: the event date, the event type, and words from the opportunity's name.
  • Anything unclear goes to a review queue with ranked candidates and plain-language reasons. One click to confirm, undo available.

Light technical grounding (skim only)

  • Native Apex inside Salesforce. No third-party cloud touches the mail. Matching walks WTA's own opportunity fields (Client Contact, Planner Contact, Billing Contact).
  • Each message is stored as a record linked to the opportunity, plus an entry on the opportunity's activity timeline. Storage math was verified against WTA's production org read-only: decades of headroom at their volume.
  • Auto-match fires only when enough independent signals agree, and that bar is a setting. Junk replies are filtered, visibly, with a one-click way back.
  • Texts arrive through a secured endpoint. Each delivery is verified against Twilio's account key, a secret only Twilio and the org share, so only genuine Twilio deliveries are accepted.
