Work Product Description

A custom online quoting and ordering application (the "Storefront") for Client's existing WordPress website, connected to Client's Salesforce org. The Storefront lets a customer choose a product and decoration, see the price change as they make choices, upload artwork, and place an order. Every order is recorded in Client's Salesforce org, which is the system of record for products, pricing, and orders. Client's team reviews each order in Salesforce, approves the comp with the customer, and collects payment through Client's existing card processor. Order entry into Client's production system (ShopWorks) remains a manual step performed by Client's team, as it is today.

02

Functional Requirements

  • A customer must be able to open the quoting page on Client's website in the current versions of

Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari on desktop and mobile, without an account, and see product, decoration, quantity by size, color-count, and add-on choices with the price per piece and total updating as each choice changes. Extended sizes carry the upcharges Client maintains in Salesforce. More than one product may be added to a single order.

  • The price shown on the website must equal the price Client's Salesforce quoting tool calculates for

the same inputs. Great Blue will verify this against the same test scenarios used for the Salesforce quoting tool. Any difference found on a received order must be reported to Client by email.

  • Pricing, products, colorways, add-ons, and preset cost values must be maintained by Client in

Salesforce only, and the website must reflect changes after the next scheduled publish, without code changes.

  • Quantity tiers must begin at a 24-piece minimum and extend the price-break structure of Client's

existing pricing model to lower quantities; quantities above Client's configured ceiling must be routed to a custom-quote request.

  • The customer must be able to upload an artwork file, see it placed on the product image, choose

one or more print locations, move, resize, and rotate it within the print area for that location on desktop and on a touch screen, add design notes, and select ink colors, and the file, locations, placement, and notes must be stored with the order in Salesforce. Additional locations are priced by the same rules as Client's Salesforce quoting tool. Accepted file types and the size limit will be set with Client during implementation.

  • Checkout must collect contact information, shipping address, requested delivery date, and the

customer's artwork-rights confirmation, create the order in Salesforce, notify Client's team, and send the customer a confirmation email. No payment is taken at checkout.

  • Checkout must show sales tax and shipping cost, calculated from the shipping address, before the

order is placed.

  • Each web order must appear in a Salesforce review queue with its line items, artwork, and customer

details, and Client's team must be able to produce the order form used for ShopWorks entry from it.

  • Client's team must be able to attach the comp to the order and send the customer a link to

a comp page showing the comp, line-item pricing by size, and the total, with approve and request-changes actions. A change request must return the order to Client's team with the customer's notes and re-price any change; an approval must open payment. The customer must receive reminder emails while a comp awaits approval, at intervals Client sets.

  • Payment must be collected after comp approval through Client's Paya Connect gateway, with card

entry on the processor's hosted form. The payment confirmation and reference must be stored on the Salesforce order and visible to Client's team. Refunds, voids, and disputes are handled by Client in the processor's own tools.

  • The customer confirmation email, the comp email, and the payment receipt must carry Client's

branding and wording approved by Client during implementation.

  • The Storefront must run on Client's existing WordPress site and hosting plan and must not require

a change of theme, page builder, or host. Deployment to Client's site is performed by Great Blue through the hosting provider's supported deployment tools, first to a staging copy for Client's review and then to the live site on Client's approval.

  • Client will provide and maintain: administrator access to the WordPress site and a current hosting

plan that supports plugins and staging; a Paya Connect gateway account with test credentials; Salesforce access; the curated product list with descriptions and a front and back photo for each colorway; the print-area position and size for each location on each product; the list of add-on options offered online; standard turnaround wording; notification addresses; the tax-rate service account; the shipping-rate service account or the shipping rate table; product weights and the ship-from address; and approval of page copy, email wording, and the comp page layout.

  • User acceptance testing will verify each Functional Requirement in this Schedule on the staging

copy of Client's site using representative products and Client's actual pricing. Acceptance requires successful completion of all material Functional Requirements, subject to the acceptance, defect-cure, and deemed-acceptance procedures in the Agreement.

  • During implementation, Great Blue will make configuration adjustments to page copy, email wording,

tier boundaries, and publish timing as needed to conform to this Schedule. Requests introducing new decoration methods, products beyond the curated list's structure, integrations, or functionality not listed in this Schedule are outside the Work Product and available under a separate scope and estimate.
